    Why build in Bangladesh

    Competitive yards, real export history, and a partner who knows them

    Bangladesh has built and exported vessels for more than fifteen years. The opportunity for owners is real. The risk is choosing the wrong yard or the wrong contract. That is the part we manage.

    Request a yard shortlist →
    Cost

    Steelwork and outfitting are labour-intensive, and Bangladeshi yards price them well below most East Asian and European builders for small and mid-size vessels.

    Track record

    Bangladeshi yards have delivered to owners in Europe, Africa and Asia since 2008, under the supervision of international classification societies.

    Proof

    Our own current work includes vessels for Kenyan public authorities and a carrier series for Dutch owners.

    Support

    Government policy backs the sector, including Bangladesh Bank refinance schemes for shipbuilding.

    Our role

    We sit on your side of the table: we match the yard to the vessel, secure the contract and payment terms, and keep a resident team on site until delivery.

    The process

    Seven stages, one accountable team

    1. Enquiry & requirements

      We start with your operating profile: trade route, cargo or service, flag, class society, budget and delivery window. You get an early view on feasibility and an indicative price band.

    2. Concept & specification

      Our naval architects prepare or review the outline specification, general arrangement and main equipment list, so that every yard quotes against the same vessel.

    3. Yard matching & tender

      We shortlist Bangladeshi yards with the right berth, experience and financial standing, run the quotation round and compare price, schedule and capability side by side.

    4. Contract, finance & guarantees

      We negotiate the shipbuilding contract with you: payment milestones, liquidated damages, the maker's list, and payment security such as bank refund guarantees, all settled before steel is cut.

    5. Design approval & procurement

      We follow class plan approval, check the yard's working drawings and manage the supply chain for long-lead items such as main engines, gearboxes and deck machinery.

    6. Construction supervision

      A resident site team inspects steelwork, welding, coating and outfitting, witnesses tests with class, and sends you regular progress and milestone reports with photographs.

    7. Trials, delivery & warranty

      We witness sea trials, check the delivery documentation and certificates, manage handover to your crew and stay on call through the guarantee period.
